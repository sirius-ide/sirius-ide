# Sirius, beside your code

Sirius has its own panel in the **secondary side bar**, on the right of the window.

- Show or hide the secondary side bar with **Ctrl+Alt+B** (**Cmd+Option+B** on macOS).
- Drag the Sirius icon to the activity bar on the left if you prefer it there.
- **Open in New Tab**, at the top of the panel, gives Sirius an editor tab of its own.
