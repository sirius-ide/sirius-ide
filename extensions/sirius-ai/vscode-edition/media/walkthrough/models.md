# Your models, your keys

Open **Models and Keys** with the gear at the top of the panel.

- **Hosted:** Anthropic, Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral and xAI. Paste your key and save it; Sirius checks it with the vendor and lists the models it offers.
- **Local:** Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp are found at their usual addresses. Give another address if yours runs elsewhere.
- **Your own server:** any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, by its address.

Keys are kept in your system keyring and are sent only to their own vendor. They are never written to your settings and never reach the chat's page.
